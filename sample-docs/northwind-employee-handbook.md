# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook

Version 3.2. Applies to all full-time employees of Northwind Labs Pvt. Ltd. (a fictional company used for this demo).

## 1. Welcome and company values

Northwind Labs builds workflow software for small and mid-sized businesses. We have offices in Bengaluru and Pune, and about 180 employees.

Our four values are:

- **Customers first.** Every decision starts with what helps the customer.
- **Own the outcome.** If you start something, you see it through to production.
- **Write it down.** Decisions, designs and meeting notes are documented in the team wiki.
- **Kind and direct.** Give honest feedback, with respect.

## 2. Before your first day

New joiners must complete these steps before their first working day:

1. Sign the offer letter and the confidentiality agreement in the HR portal.
2. Upload a government photo ID and your PAN card for payroll.
3. Submit bank account details for salary credit.
4. Choose a laptop: a 14-inch MacBook Pro or a Lenovo ThinkPad T14. IT ships it to your home address 3 working days before you start.
5. Set up two-factor authentication on your company Google account using the link in your welcome email.

On day one you'll meet your manager and your onboarding buddy. Your buddy is a teammate who answers everyday questions during your first 60 days.

## 3. Working hours and attendance

Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours you may plan your own schedule, as long as you work 40 hours per week.

Attendance is recorded automatically when you sign in to the HR portal. If you will be unavailable during core hours, update your status in Slack and tell your manager.

## 4. Leave policy

### 4.1 Annual leave

Full-time employees receive **24 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year, credited at 2 days per month. Up to 10 unused days can be carried over to the next year; anything above 10 days lapses on 31 December.

Leave of 3 or more consecutive days must be requested in the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance. Shorter leave needs 2 working days' notice.

### 4.2 Sick leave

Employees receive **12 days of paid sick leave** per year. Sick leave cannot be carried over. A doctor's certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.

### 4.3 Public holidays

Northwind observes 10 public holidays per year. The list for each office is published in the HR portal every December.

### 4.4 Parental leave

Birthing parents receive 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Non-birthing parents receive 4 weeks of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption.

## 5. Remote and hybrid work

Northwind is hybrid. Teams agree their own office days, with a minimum of **2 days per week in the office** for employees living within 40 km of an office.

Employees who live more than 40 km from an office may work fully remotely with their manager's approval.

### 5.1 Working from another country

You may work from outside India for up to **30 calendar days per year**. You must get written approval from your manager and the People team at least 3 weeks before travel, because of tax and data-security rules. Customer data must never be accessed from countries on the restricted list in the security policy.

## 6. Equipment and expenses

Each employee receives a one-time **home-office allowance of ₹15,000** to buy a chair, monitor or other equipment. Claim it within your first 90 days by uploading receipts in the expense tool.

Internet costs are reimbursed up to ₹1,000 per month.

Work travel must be booked through the travel desk. Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours. Hotel limits are ₹6,000 per night in metro cities and ₹4,000 elsewhere.

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of spending, with receipts. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary.

## 7. Learning and growth

Every employee has an annual **learning budget of ₹40,000** for courses, books, certifications and conferences. Spending above ₹10,000 at once needs your manager's approval.

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Salary revisions are made once a year, effective 1 April.

## 8. Security and IT

- Lock your screen whenever you leave your laptop.
- Never share passwords; use the company password manager.
- Report a lost or stolen laptop to it-help@northwind.example within 1 hour.
- Do not install unapproved software on company laptops. Request new tools through the IT portal.
- Customer data must stay in approved systems and must never be copied to personal devices.

## 9. Code of conduct

Northwind has zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination. Concerns can be raised with your manager, the People team, or anonymously through the ethics hotline. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is itself a violation of this code.

## 10. Leaving Northwind

The notice period is **60 days** for all employees after confirmation, and 15 days during probation. Probation lasts 6 months.

On your last day, return your laptop and access card to IT. Your full and final settlement is paid within 45 days of your last working day.
